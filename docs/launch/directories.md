**Blurb (60 words):** Unwind Words rewrites the words that stop dyslexic readers — heteronyms,
re-used spellings, sound-alikes, tangled sentences — reversibly, with a friendly respelling
above hard words and read-aloud that highlights each word. A ten-minute screening tunes it to
you. Free to read; Pro converts whole books to dyslexia-friendly EPUB and sends them to your
Kindle. Open source.
