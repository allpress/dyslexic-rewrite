**Name:** Unwind Words

**Tagline (60 chars):** Books rewritten so dyslexic readers can actually read them

**Description:**

Unwind Words finds the words that stop a dyslexic reader cold — "wind" that becomes "wind"
two lines later, phrasal verbs that mislead, sound-alikes landing next to each other, long
tangled sentences — and rewrites them, reversibly. Every change is one tap from the original.
Hard words get a friendly respelling right above them (in-TEN-shun), and everything can be read
aloud with the word highlighted.

A ten-minute screening builds your profile across five reading axes, and the rewrites change
with it. Paste anything, import a web page, or (Pro) upload a whole book — EPUB, PDF, DOCX —
and get back a dyslexia-friendly EPUB you can send straight to your Kindle.

The engine is open source (MIT). The reading tools are free. Pro ($5/month) is for whole
books and pays the hosting bill.

Built by a developer for his wife. Not a diagnosis, not a cure — a tool that makes the next
page easier.

**Topics:** Accessibility, Education, Open Source, Productivity, Reading
