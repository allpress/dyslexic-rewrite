# Demo videos (30–60 s, screen recording, captions on, no face needed)

Record at 1080×1920 for Shorts/Reels/TikTok; the site is fine at phone width. Big text: set
the reader to 24 px before recording. One idea per video. Post the same file to TikTok,
YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels; link in bio → sample chapter.

1. **"Why *wind* stops a dyslexic reader cold"** — Show the Willows paragraph with "wind …
   wind". Tap the second one: WIND (rhymes with pinned). Tap the first: WYND. Caption: same
   spelling, two words. Your brain has to decide twice. Now it doesn't. (30 s)
2. **"Watch a paragraph get unwound"** — Paste a dense paragraph (Treasure Island's first
   sentence, 90 words). Press Rewrite. Split into four sentences; hover a changed word to show
   the original. Caption: nothing deleted, everything reversible. (40 s)
3. **"Ten minutes, five axes"** — Skip through the screening quickly, land on the radar.
   Caption: not a type, not a diagnosis — a picture of what helps you. (45 s)
4. **"Book → Kindle"** — Upload an EPUB, watch progress, press Send to Kindle, cut to the
   Kindle showing ruby respellings. Caption: any book, your rules. (45 s)
5. **"Read-aloud that keeps your place"** — Turn on TTS, words highlight as they're spoken,
   spotlight dims the rest. (30 s)
6. **"Any web page"** — The extension: Alt+U on a news article → overlay reader. (30 s)
7. **"What the research says (and doesn't)"** — Text-on-screen: fonts: no. Overlays: no.
   Shorter, commoner words: yes. Read-aloud: yes. Rewriting for *you*: we're measuring it.
   (30 s) — this one gets shared by educators.

Caption footer on every video: Free to try, open source. unwindwords.com
