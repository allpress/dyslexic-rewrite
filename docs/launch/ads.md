# Paid ads — one channel, one month, $100

Only after two weeks of organic traffic so there is a baseline in `/api/admin/stats`. Land
every ad on the sample chapter or the screening, never the pricing page; the free experience
is the conversion path (one free book → Pro).

## Test 1: Reddit Ads ($100, 30 days)

- Objective: traffic. Targeting: interests "Dyslexia", "ADHD", "Accessibility"; communities
  r/dyslexia, r/ADHD, r/Teachers. US/UK/CA/AU. Desktop + mobile.
- Landing: https://unwindwords.com/read?sample=wind-in-the-willows?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=sample
  (the counters record referrer host; UTM is for your own reading of the URL)
- Creative A (image: the before/after strip):
  **Headline:** The word that stops you reading — fixed.
  **Body:** Unwind Words rewrites the words that trip dyslexic readers and keeps the original
  one tap away. Free. Open source. Try a chapter, no sign-up.
- Creative B (image: phonetic map on "wind"):
  **Headline:** WYND or WIND? Now the page tells you.
  **Body:** A friendly respelling above every hard word, read-aloud that highlights as it
  goes, and rewrites tuned to how you read. Free to try.
- Creative C (image: the radar):
  **Headline:** Which kind of reader are you?
  **Body:** Ten minutes, five plain-language axes, no diagnosis — and the text rewrites itself
  to match. Free.

## Test 2: Google Search ($100, 30 days) — only if test 1 beats organic

Exact/phrase keywords: "dyslexia friendly books", "dyslexia reading app", "text simplifier
for dyslexia", "make text easier to read dyslexia", "dyslexia kindle". Negative: "test for
children", "font download". One responsive search ad using the three headlines above.

## What "works" means

Cost per newsletter signup or account under $2, and at least one Pro subscriber attributable
to the campaign in the month. If neither, stop paid ads and put the $100 into a month of
weekly classics and demo videos instead.
