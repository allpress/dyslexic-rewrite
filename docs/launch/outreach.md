# Outreach to organisations and writers

One email each, personal, short, no attachments. Send from hello@unwindwords.com. Follow up
once after ten days; then stop.

## Template

Subject: A free, open-source reading tool for dyslexic adults — for your <resources page>

Hi <name>,

I'm Doug Allpress. My wife Jennifer is dyslexic, and I built a tool for her that I'd like you
to consider for <the "assistive technology" page / your article on free tools>.

Unwind Words rewrites the specific words that stop dyslexic readers — one spelling read two
ways, sound-alikes next to each other, tangled sentences — and keeps the original one tap
away. Hard words get a plain respelling above them. There's a ten-minute screening that gives
a five-axis profile (multiple-deficit model, not a "type") and tunes the rewriting. The
reading tools are free with no account; the engine is open source (MIT). It does not claim to
diagnose or cure anything, and we don't make the font/overlay claims the evidence doesn't
support — our research summary with citations is public.

Try a chapter without signing in: https://unwindwords.com/read?sample=wind-in-the-willows
Research and evidence notes: https://github.com/allpress/dyslexic-rewrite/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH.md

If it's not a fit for the page, I'd still value one sentence on why.

Thank you,
Doug
